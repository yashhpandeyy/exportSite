About EverHorizon Exim

A young paper house built on an old craft.

EverHorizon Exim is a proprietorship paper manufacturing and trading business based in Mandropur, Mahesana district, Gujarat — a state long associated with India's paper and packaging belt. We produce 80 GSM virgin paper in jumbo roll form and convert it into premium cut sheets, from standard A4 to sizes specified by the customer.

We registered under GST as a Regular taxpayer in November 2025, formalising a business built on a simple premise: paper is a commodity everyone needs and few suppliers get consistently right on grammage, whiteness, and delivery timelines. We set out to be the supplier that does.

Our Aim

Consistent grammage. Honest pricing. No surprises at the dock.

Our aim is straightforward: to become a dependable source of virgin paper for buyers who are tired of variance between batches. Every roll and every ream we ship is meant to measure exactly what the invoice says it measures.

Global Supply

We export. Distance is not a limitation.

EverHorizon Exim is built to serve buyers beyond India as much as within it. Our name is our thesis — paper stock that moves past every horizon, not just the domestic market.

  • 01

    Documentation, handled

    Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and shipping documentation prepared for every export order.

  • 02

    Flexible Incoterms

    We quote on FOB, CIF, or CFR terms from Indian ports, depending on what suits your logistics arrangement.

  • 03

    Container-ready packing

    Rolls are core-wrapped and reams shrink-packed to withstand sea freight, with palletisation available on request.

  • 04

    Custom specification runs

    Roll width, core diameter, GSM, and cut size can all be adjusted to match the standards of the destination market.
