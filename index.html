About EverHorizon Exim

A modern Exim enterprise rooted in India's commercial hub.

EverHorizon Exim is a proprietorship trading and export enterprise headquartered in Mandropur, Mahesana district, Gujarat. Operating at the heart of western India's prominent petrochemical, agricultural, and paper manufacturing corridors, we bridge industrial buyers worldwide with reliable Indian manufacturing capacity.

Whether you require standard 80 GSM paper rolls, PLB HDPE duct pipes for telecom networks, chopped glass fibers, or bulk agricultural staples like Sortex Jeera and dal, we operate with a singular commitment: deliver the stated specification at the most competitive price in the market.
